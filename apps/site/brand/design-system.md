# Design system — Astroidal

PATTERN: Single-page product landing. Hero with the real recording, then the review path, two recorded maps, the score rule, the setup prompt, questions, and a closing prompt.
STYLE: The constellation.html theme. OLED night, panel surfaces, mono eyebrows, sans headlines.
COLORS:

- Background: #0B0F19
- Panel: #0C111C
- Text: #E8EEF8
- Muted: #A8B3C7
- Primary / Passed: #00F2FE
- Nebula: #7F00FF
- Iris: #D2B4FF
- Fix first: #FFB020

TYPOGRAPHY: IBM Plex Sans for headlines and body. JetBrains Mono for eyebrows, scores, paths, and commands.
MOOD: An observatory for a diff. Quiet, high contrast, one glow used with restraint.
KEY EFFECTS: Radial nebula in the page background, a slow decorative sky, cyan focus rings, 160ms border and color transitions.
ANTI-PATTERNS: Light theme, generic purple SaaS gradients, invented customer quotes, emoji icons, Inter or Roboto.
